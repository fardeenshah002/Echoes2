# ECHOES - Pro Audio Suite 🎧

A sleek, modern web-based music streaming application with a responsive YouTube Music-inspired interface and Deep Space & Neon theme.

## ✨ Features Added in this Update:
- **Smooth Page Scrolling**: Fixed height constraints allowing complete vertical scroll across Home, Explore, and Library views.
- **5+ Horizontal Swipe Carousels**:
  1. *Made for You* (Smart personalized recommendations based on play history)
  2. *Recommended Curated Playlists* (Interactive curated mixes with Play All)
  3. *Trending Today* (Global hit tracks)
  4. *Chill & Lo-Fi Lounge* (Mellow study & focus beats)
  5. *High Energy Workout* (Fast-paced gym and workout phonk)
  6. *Bollywood & Desi Melodies* (Chart-topping romance & acoustic)
- **Functional Playlist Modals**: Clicking any recommended playlist opens the list of tracks and lets you stream instantly.
- **Custom Playlists & Liked Songs**: Manage playlists saved in `localStorage`.
- **Live Synced Lyrics & Micro-animations**: Real-time line highlighting and auto-scrolling lyrics.
